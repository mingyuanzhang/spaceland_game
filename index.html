How to play

You float, with nothing pulling you anywhere. Find the mint-green light and fly into it. After each escape you watch your whole route from outside.

W A S D
fly forward and back, strafe left and right
Space / R, Shift / F
rise and sink, relative to your own head
Mouse or arrows

Q / E
roll
T

Esc or P
pause, restart, or give up and see your path

Every wall keeps its own color, so familiar paint means you have been here. Green light spills along corridors near the exit and bends around corners, but never passes through a wall. The ball in the corner is a compass fixed to the world: the letter at its center is the direction you face.

Later worlds close on themselves: fly far enough and you come back from the other side. If you see a small tetrahedron ahead of you, you are looking at your own back.
